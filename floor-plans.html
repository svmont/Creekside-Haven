Floor plan questions

Can I see the actual home before I sign?

Yes. If the home you want is vacant and ready, we will walk you through that exact unit — not a lookalike. If it is still occupied or being turned, we will show you the same floor plan and tell you when yours will be ready.

Is a washer and dryer included?

Yes. Every home has a dedicated laundry room off the living area with a full-size washer and dryer included — not a stacked unit in a hallway closet.

Can I choose my building or floor?

Where we have more than one home open in the plan you want, yes — tell your leasing specialist whether you would rather be upstairs or on the ground floor and we will show you what is available.

Is there covered parking?

Yes — covered parking is available at the community. Ask us about current availability and the monthly charge.
