Floor plan questions

Can I see a specific home before I sign?

Yes — once a home is released for leasing you are welcome to walk it. During pre-leasing we tour the model and the matching plan, then you reserve the exact building and floor you want.

Is there room for a full-size washer and dryer?

Yes. Both plans have a dedicated 6-by-3-foot laundry room off the living area with full-size connections — not a stacked closet in the hallway.

Which plans have the best views?

Homes along the greenbelt and creek side look out over trees rather than parking. Those release first — tell your leasing specialist if that matters to you.

Do you offer garages?

Detached garages and covered parking are available for an additional monthly charge, assigned first come, first served. Open surface parking is included.
