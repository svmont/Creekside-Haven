Why Creekside Haven

Comfortable living, without the crowd.

Creekside Haven offers spacious one- and two-bedroom homes in a smaller 48-home community, with everyday conveniences nearby and easy access to Conroe and The Woodlands.

Our one-bedroom homes offer 715 square feet and our two-bedrooms offer 975, with dedicated dining areas, in-home laundry and generous storage.

Quieter living

With just 48 homes across eight two-story buildings, Creekside Haven feels more personal and less crowded.

Convenient location

Frequently asked questions.

When can I move in?

Homes come available throughout the year, so it depends on what has just been turned. Send us a note and we will tell you exactly what is open now and what is coming up.

Are pets welcome?

Pets are considered, with fees, weight limits and breed restrictions set by management. Ask us for the current pet policy before you apply.

What schools serve the community?

Creekside Haven is located in Montgomery ISD. Campus assignments are set by the district and can change — confirm your address with Montgomery ISD before you sign.

What is included in rent?

Trash and pest control are included. Residents set up electricity and internet; water and sewer are billed monthly. Ask us for a full breakdown for the home you are considering.

Do you offer short-term leases?

Lease terms from 7 to 15 months are available, priced by term and availability.
