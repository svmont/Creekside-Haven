The neighborhood

Montgomery, Texas —
small‑town charm,
everyday convenience.

Creekside Haven puts you close to the best of Montgomery — everyday shopping, local dining, Lake Conroe and historic downtown — with easy access to Conroe and The Woodlands.

  • Everyday essentials nearby

    H-E-B, Kroger, Walmart, Walgreens and dining, all minutes away.

  • Historic Downtown Montgomery

    Restaurants, coffee and shops around a walkable historic square.

  • Lake Conroe

    Boating, waterfront dining and recreation, about 12 minutes away.

  • Easy access

    TX-105, TX-249 and I-45 make Conroe and The Woodlands easy to reach.

Getting around

Everything you need, within easy reach.

TX-249 opened up the run south, and I-45 handles everything north. Whether you work in The Woodlands, Conroe or at the kitchen table, the commute stays reasonable.

Neighborhood questions

What school district is Creekside Haven in?

Montgomery ISD. Specific campus assignments are determined by the district and can change from year to year — verify the address with Montgomery ISD directly.

How far is the nearest grocery store?

About five minutes. The H-E-B on TX-105 and Kroger Marketplace are both a short drive, along with pharmacies, banks and everyday retail.

Is this a reasonable commute to The Woodlands?

Most residents get to The Woodlands in roughly 25 minutes via FM 149 and TX-249, and to Conroe or the I-45 corridor in about 20.

What is there to do nearby?

Lake Conroe for boating and lakeside dining, golf courses within a few minutes, state park trails, and the restaurants, shops and festivals of historic downtown Montgomery.

Best way to judge a neighborhood? Drive it.
