Neighborhood FAQ

What school district is Creekside Haven in?

Creekside Haven is located in Montgomery ISD and is currently zoned to Montgomery Elementary, Montgomery Junior High and Montgomery High School. School assignments are determined by the district, so confirm current zoning directly with Montgomery ISD.

How close are groceries and everyday shopping?

H-E-B is about five minutes away, with Kroger Marketplace, Walmart, pharmacies, banks and other everyday shopping nearby.

How far are Conroe and The Woodlands?

Conroe is approximately 20 minutes away, while The Woodlands is approximately 25 minutes away. Drive times vary with traffic.
